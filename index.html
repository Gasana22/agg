About Our Farm

Nestled in the heart of the countryside, AGG FARM is a family-owned farm dedicated to sustainable agriculture and animal welfare. Our 580-acre property features lush pastures for our dairy cows, thriving eucalyptus plantations, and a natural water dam that supports our ecosystem.

Goat Farming

We raise a herd of 150 goats with a focus on ethical treatment and natural grazing. Our goats produce high-quality milk and cheese products while maintaining the health of our pastures.

Cattle Ranch

Our grass-fed cattle enjoy open pastures and access to fresh water from our dam. We practice rotational grazing to maintain pasture health and provide the highest quality beef.

Dairy

Our pasture-raised dairy cows produce rich, wholesome milk that forms the foundation for all our dairy products. We follow ethical farming practices and minimal processing to preserve nutritional quality.

Eucalyptus Plantation

Our 50-acre eucalyptus plantation serves multiple purposes - providing shade for animals, producing essential oils, and contributing to sustainable forestry practices.

Our Sustainability Practices

Waste Management

We implement comprehensive composting and recycling programs to minimize landfill waste and return nutrients to our soil.

Water Conservation

Our rainwater harvesting system and efficient irrigation practices reduce water usage by 40% compared to conventional farms.

Renewable Energy

Solar panels provide 80% of our energy needs, reducing our carbon footprint and energy costs.

Regenerative Grazing

Rotational grazing practices improve soil health, sequester carbon, and increase biodiversity.

Agroforestry

Our integrated tree planting provides habitat for wildlife, prevents erosion, and creates microclimates.

Organic Practices

We avoid synthetic pesticides and fertilizers, relying instead on natural alternatives and biological controls.
