Our Sustainable Process

Planting & Growth

We implement sustainable planting techniques with proper spacing and soil preparation. Our trees grow rapidly, reaching maturity in just 7-10 years.

Maintenance

Our selective pruning and thinning program focuses on tree health and optimal growth. We use natural methods to maintain soil fertility.

Harvesting

We follow sustainable harvesting protocols that ensure continuous forest cover while maximizing timber yield and oil production.

Processing

From timber milling to oil distillation, we process all materials on-site with minimal waste and maximum efficiency.
