Our Dairy Process

Pasture Grazing

Our cows enjoy fresh pasture daily through rotational grazing, which ensures optimal nutrition and animal welfare while maintaining healthy grasslands.

Gentle Milking

Twice daily milking in our modern parlor with strict hygiene protocols to ensure the highest quality milk from healthy, comfortable cows.

Rapid Cooling

Milk is immediately cooled to 38°F and transported to our processing facility within hours to preserve freshness and nutritional value.

Minimal Processing

We use low-temperature pasteurization and avoid homogenization to maintain the natural flavor and health benefits of our milk.
