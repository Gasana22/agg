Our Water Dam

The heart of our farm's sustainable water management system, providing irrigation, wildlife habitat, and natural beauty.

Sustainable Water Management

Our 5-acre dam was constructed in 2012 and has since become a thriving ecosystem that supports our farming operations and local wildlife:

  • Primary irrigation source for pastures via solar-powered pumps
  • Stocked with bass and bluegill for natural pest control
  • Certified wildlife habitat by the National Wildlife Federation
  • Emergency water supply for fire prevention
  • Recreational fishing (by appointment)

We practice careful water conservation and have implemented a drip irrigation system to maximize efficiency. Annual water testing ensures quality for both livestock and wildlife.

Eucalyptus Plantation

Sustainable forestry that benefits our farm ecosystem while providing valuable resources.

Multi-Purpose Forestry

We manage our 40-acre eucalyptus plantation with these sustainable goals:

  • Windbreak to protect animals and crops from harsh winds
  • Sustainable timber production (7-year rotation cycle)
  • Habitat for pollinators and beneficial insects
  • Carbon sequestration - estimated 50 tons annually
  • Erosion control on sloping areas of the farm

We offer eucalyptus poles for fencing, firewood, and essential oil production materials. All harvesting is done sustainably with replanting.
