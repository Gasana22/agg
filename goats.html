Our Sustainable Process

Pasture Management

We implement rotational grazing across our 200-acre pasture system, allowing vegetation to recover while providing our goats with diverse, nutrient-rich forage. Our pastures are never treated with synthetic herbicides or pesticides.

Breeding Program

Our selective breeding program focuses on health, temperament, and production traits. We work with veterinary geneticists to maintain the highest quality bloodlines while improving disease resistance and feed efficiency.

Animal Care

From birth to maturity, our goats receive individualized care. Our protocols include regular health checks, hoof trimming, and preventative treatments using natural remedies whenever possible.

Harvesting

For our dairy goats, we follow strict milking protocols to ensure hygiene and animal comfort. Our meat goats are processed at USDA-certified facilities with the highest standards of humane treatment.
