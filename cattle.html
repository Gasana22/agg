Our Cattle Farming Philosophy

Our Sustainable Process

Pasture Management

We implement rotational grazing across our 500-acre pasture system, allowing vegetation to recover while providing our cattle with diverse, nutrient-rich forage. Our pastures are never treated with synthetic herbicides or pesticides.

Breeding Program

Our selective breeding program focuses on health, temperament, and meat quality traits. We work with veterinary geneticists to maintain the highest quality bloodlines while improving feed efficiency and marbling characteristics.

Animal Care

From birth to maturity, our cattle receive individualized care. Our protocols include regular health checks, low-stress handling techniques, and preventative treatments using natural remedies whenever possible.

Processing

Our cattle are processed at USDA-certified facilities with the highest standards of humane treatment. We use dry-aging techniques to enhance flavor and tenderness, resulting in superior beef quality.
